The three things stopping you

"I don't have time for a weekly call."

Backwards, and I say it with sympathy because I believed it too. Think about last month: how many hours went into a decision you circled for three weeks and could have settled in ten minutes with someone who'd already made it? You're paying that tax every week right now. And if life takes a week from you — it will — the group and the community are still there when you're back.

"I'm not really a group person."

Neither was I. I joined my first mastermind certain I'd get more done alone, and I was wrong in a way that cost me years. The problem you can't see is, by definition, the one you can't see. Everybody else spots it in about four minutes. And half of what I know I learned by listening to somebody else get helped.

"I'm honestly doing okay on my own."

I believe you. But listen to the sentence again. Okay. On my own. "Okay" is the most dangerous number in business — bad forces a change, good funds a change, and okay just quietly continues until something outside you decides it's finished. You've been okay for years. That's exactly the problem.
